# Monitoring

> **Purpose:** how you find out the site is down, broken or not receiving messages.
> **Filled by:** WEB-34 (step 12)
> **Applies to:** every site

**Status:** Empty

## After every deploy

*Open the live site and the page you changed. Netlify can email you when a deploy fails: switch on deploy notifications.*

## Every month

*Send a test message through the form and check it arrives. Delete form messages older than the time set in DATA-PRIVACY.md, from Netlify Forms and from your inbox. Check Google Search Console for errors. Run WEB-36.*

## Uptime check (optional)

*A free uptime service can email you if the site goes down. If you add one, record which, and check it collects no visitor data.*

## Analytics

*None by default. Before adding any, read the verify box in step 11 of the guide and update DATA-PRIVACY.md.*
