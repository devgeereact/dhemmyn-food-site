# Email spec

> **Purpose:** how form messages reach you, and the email settings the site depends on.
> **Filled by:** WEB-34 (step 12)
> **Applies to:** every site with a form or an email link

**Status:** Empty

## Form messages

*Form name, the host that receives it, and the address that gets an email notification for each message.*

## Spam

*The host's spam filter and the hidden spam trap field. Check the host's spam list now and then for real messages.*

## Replying

*Who replies, how quickly, and from which address.*

## Business email on your domain

*If your email uses your domain, its MX records must never be changed when connecting the site to the host.*

## Test

*Date of the last test message, and whether it arrived.*
