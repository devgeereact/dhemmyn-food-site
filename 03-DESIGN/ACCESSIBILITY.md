# Accessibility

> **Purpose:** how the site works for everyone, including keyboard and screen reader users, plus the alt text for every photo.
> **Filled by:** WEB-18 (step 7)
> **Applies to:** every site

**Status:** Empty

## Target

*WCAG 2.2 level AA.*

## Keyboard and focus

*Every link, button and field reachable with Tab in reading order, with a visible focus outline. A skip to content link. Escape closes the menu.*

## Colour and contrast

*The ratios from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md. Colour is never the only way something is shown.*

## Text and zoom

*Text can be zoomed to 200 per cent and the page still works at 320 pixels wide with no sideways scrolling.*

## Forms

*Visible labels, required fields marked in words, errors described in text next to the field and announced to screen readers.*

## Page changes

*After moving to another page, the page starts at the top and the new page is announced or focused.*

## Motion

*Respects the reduce motion setting. Nothing flashes.*

## Structure

*One main heading per page, headings in order, and header, navigation, main and footer areas marked up.*

## Photos and alt text

*Alt text of 8 to 15 words that says what matters to a visitor. Decorative pictures get empty alt text. Never start with "image of".*
