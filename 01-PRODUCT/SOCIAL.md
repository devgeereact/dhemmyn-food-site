# Social media

> **Purpose:** the business's social accounts, the WhatsApp link, and how posts appear on the site.
> **Filled by:** WEB-06 (step 4) and WEB-27 (step 9)
> **Applies to:** every site with social media

**Status:** Empty

*Plain links set no cookies. Embed scripts from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube never load without consent: use the business's own images linking out, or a click-to-load placeholder. Never record passwords here.*

## WhatsApp

*Click-to-chat link: https://wa.me/ followed by the mobile number in international form, with no plus sign, no first 0 and no spaces (07700 900123 becomes 447700900123). Only a number the owner confirms is for the business.*

## Embeds

*Usually none. If a video or post must be embedded, it sits behind a click-to-load placeholder that says which company it loads from.*

## Owner actions

*For example: send 6 to 9 post images and links; switch Instagram to a Business or Creator account and authorise Behold once (feed only); update the grid when there is a new favourite post.*
