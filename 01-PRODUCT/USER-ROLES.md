# User roles

## Visitors

*Each kind of visitor on its own line: who they are, what they want, which device they probably use. For example: a parent planning a birthday, on a phone, wants prices and how to order.*

## The owner

*Who looks after the site, how they update it (through the AI assistant), and how often.*

## Other people

*Anyone else with a part to play, such as someone who answers enquiries. Write "None" if there is nobody.*

## Log-ins

*Usually: none. Nobody logs in to this site. If that changes, fill 05-SECURITY/AUTH-MODEL.md and PERMISSIONS.md.*
