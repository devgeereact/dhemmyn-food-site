# Sync spec

> **Purpose:** how work done offline is kept and sent later, without loss or duplicates.
> **Filled by:** WEB-19 (step 8)
> **Applies to:** PWA only, and only if visitors can do things offline that must reach you later

**Status:** Not applicable

Not applicable: nothing is saved offline and sent later. A form that cannot send shows the failed message and keeps what was typed.
